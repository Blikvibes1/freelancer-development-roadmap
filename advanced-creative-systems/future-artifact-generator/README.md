# Relic — Future Artifact Generator

**Project 22 · Advanced Creative Series**

Generate plausible artifacts from 20–100 years ahead based on technological, social, and cultural trend vectors. Each artifact includes form, materials, intended use, cultural context, and unintended consequences.

---

## Purpose

Objects that feel both strange and believable — coherent futures made tangible.

---

## Features

- **Horizon** — 25 to 100 years  
- **Trend vector** — tech · social · culture · domain  
- **Artifact card** — name, silhouette, full dossier  
- **Seeded generation** — reproducible combinations  
- **Themes**: Archive · Lab · Dusk  
- Zero build step  

---

## How to Run

1. Open `index.html`.  
2. Set horizon and trend chips.  
3. **Excavate artifact**.  
4. Read form, materials, use, context, and side effects.

---

## Technical Notes

- Combinatorial banks constrained by domain and tech  
- Canvas silhouette by domain  
- No frameworks

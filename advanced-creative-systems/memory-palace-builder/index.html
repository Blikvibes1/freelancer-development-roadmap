    Art of Memory

    Principles that make loci stick.

    1. Familiar path

    Use a real place you know well — home, school, a route you walk. Memory prefers routes you can walk without thinking.

    2. Vivid & absurd

    Ordinary images fade. Make them huge, colorful, moving, smelling, sounding. Absurdity is glue.

    3. Emotion

    Attach feeling — humor, fear, beauty, disgust. The amygdala tags emotional scenes for long-term storage.

    4. One idea per locus

    Overloading a single spot creates interference. Space ideas along a clear sequence of places.

    5. Revisit

    Walk the palace soon after encoding, then at expanding intervals. Spatial recall is a skill that strengthens with use.

    6. Encode, then test

    Use the Recall mode. Struggle to retrieve is part of learning — it deepens the path.

Deep work surface

Write or think here. The interface watches rhythm—not content—and softens or steadies itself to protect your attention.

Session 0:00 Signals: idle

How it listens

  • Pointer stillness — Long pauses suggest absorption or hesitation.
  • Scroll rhythm — Smooth, periodic scroll supports flow; frantic scroll flags distraction.
  • Keystroke cadence — Steady typing deepens focus mode; bursts and stops keep neutral.
  • Idle depth — Extended idle without leave softens chrome (protect, don’t nag).

Calm

Reduced chrome, slower motion, more space. When the system senses scattered or idle attention.

Focus

Steady contrast, locked layout, minimal interruption. When rhythm is sustained and deliberate.

Flow

Fluid transitions, ambient feedback. When interaction is continuous and lightly exploratory.

No webcam. No content reading. Only behavioral rhythm—and only to protect, never to demand.
